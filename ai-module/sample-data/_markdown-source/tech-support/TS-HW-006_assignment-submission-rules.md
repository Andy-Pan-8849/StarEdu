# Assignment Submission Rules

## 1. How to submit

1. Complete the assignment in your personal course repository (see TS-ENV-004).
2. Push your final commit to the `main` branch.
3. Open the assignment in the learning portal and paste the link of the commit. The portal records the submission time.

## 2. Naming

Name archives and report files `<studentID>_<course>_<assignment>`, for example `BJ20260123_java_A04`. Submissions with other names may not be matched by the autograder.

## 3. Deadlines

The deadline is **23:59 campus time** on the due date shown in the portal. The time of the portal submission counts, not the time of the commit.

## 4. Late submissions

| Delay | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Up to 24 hours | 10 percent deducted from the score |
| 24 to 48 hours | 20 percent deducted |
| 48 to 72 hours | 30 percent deducted |
| More than 72 hours | Score of zero |

## 5. Resubmission

You may resubmit as often as you like before the deadline; the last submission counts. After the deadline a resubmission is treated as a late submission.

## 6. Extensions

An extension of up to 3 days is granted for medical reasons with a doctor's note, or for family emergencies. Request it in the portal at least 24 hours before the deadline, except for emergencies, which can be reported afterwards within 3 days.

## 7. Plagiarism and AI tools

- Copying code from another student or from the internet without a reference is plagiarism. The first offense gives a score of zero and an academic review. A second offense leads to dismissal from the program.
- AI coding assistants are allowed to explain concepts and to help debugging. If you use them for code that you submit, add an "AI usage" section to your README describing the tool and what it was used for. Undisclosed use is treated as plagiarism.

## 8. Grades and regrading

Grades are published within 5 business days after the deadline. Regrade requests must be sent to the instructor within 7 days after publication.
