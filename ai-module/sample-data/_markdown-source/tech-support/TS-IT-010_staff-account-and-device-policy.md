# Staff Account and Device Policy

This policy applies to employees only. Students must not receive this document.

## 1. Accounts

Each employee gets a company account when they join. Accounts are removed on the last working day.

## 2. Passwords and multi-factor authentication

- Passwords have at least 12 characters and are changed every 180 days.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the HR portal, the Finance portal and GitLab.
- Passwords must not be shared. Shared accounts are not allowed.

## 3. Laptops

IT issues a laptop to every employee. Instructors receive a laptop with the three course environments (TS-ENV-001 to TS-ENV-003) preinstalled. Laptops are replaced after 4 years.

## 4. Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the helpdesk within **2 hours** after you notice it. IT locks the account and erases the device remotely. A report after more than 24 hours may lead to disciplinary action.

## 5. Remote access

Staff connect to internal systems from outside the campus through the company VPN. MFA is needed on every VPN login.

## 6. Student data

Student personal data, such as grades and phone numbers, may only be used for work purposes and must not be copied to private devices or personal cloud storage. Data breaches must be reported to IT Security at once.

## 7. Offboarding

On the last working day the employee returns the laptop and access cards. IT archives the mailbox for 90 days.
