# Helpdesk Service Levels and Loaner Equipment

## 1. Contact

- Email: helpdesk@starit.example
- Phone: extension 8000 on any campus phone
- Walk-in: the IT desk on the first floor of each campus

## 2. Opening hours

The helpdesk is open from **08:00 to 22:00, Monday to Saturday**. Requests received outside these hours are handled on the next opening day.

## 3. Priorities and response times

| Priority | Example | First response | Target resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Classroom or lab outage, portal or GitLab down for everyone | 30 minutes | 4 hours |
| P2 | One person cannot sign in or submit an assignment | 4 hours | 1 business day |
| P3 | How-to question, software installation help | 1 business day | 3 business days |

Mark an urgent assignment problem as P2 and write the deadline in the ticket. Before the problem can be handled, the helpdesk may ask for the error code and a screenshot.

## 4. VPN for students

The student VPN gives access to GitLab and the package mirrors from outside the campus. Request it by email with your student ID. Access is granted within 1 business day (P3).

## 5. Loaner laptops

Students whose laptop is broken can borrow a laptop for up to **14 days**. A refundable deposit of CNY 500 is required. The laptop comes with the Java, Python and TypeScript setups of TS-ENV-001 to TS-ENV-003 installed. Late return costs CNY 20 per day.

## 6. Lab rules

Labs are open from 08:00 to 22:00. Food and drinks are not allowed. Do not install software on lab machines; use the virtual machines provided by IT.
