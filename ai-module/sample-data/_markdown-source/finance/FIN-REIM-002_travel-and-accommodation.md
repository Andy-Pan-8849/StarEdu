# Travel and Accommodation Policy

## 1. Approval

Every business trip needs approval from the Department Head before booking. Inter-campus teaching exchange trips for instructors are also approved by the Academic Office. Bookings should be made through the company travel platform.

## 2. Hotel limits

The hotel limit is per night, including taxes. Cities follow the campus tier list in HR-GEN-000. Cities that are not campus cities use the "Other cities" limit.

| City type | Limit per night (CNY) |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 (Beijing, Shanghai, Shenzhen, Guangzhou) | 600 |
| Tier 2 (Hangzhou, Chengdu, Wuhan, Xi'an) | 450 |
| Other cities | 350 |

Employees at level M3 (Campus Director) and above may exceed the limit by up to 20 percent. Any amount over the limit is paid by the employee.

## 3. Meal allowance on business trips

The meal allowance is a fixed **CNY 100 per day** for each full travel day in all cities. No meal receipts are needed, but the hotel invoice and the transport tickets must support the travel days.

## 4. Long-distance transport

| Mode | Rule |
|---|---|
| Flights | Economy class. Book at least 7 days in advance. Bookings made later need Campus Director approval. |
| High-speed rail | Second class. First class is allowed only for level M3 and above. |
| Rental car | Not allowed without VP approval. |

## 5. Local transport

Metro and bus fares are reimbursed in full. A taxi or ride-hailing trip is reimbursed when:

1. Two or more employees travel together, or
2. The trip is after 21:00, or
3. The employee carries teaching equipment or heavy luggage.

## 6. Cancellations

Non-refundable tickets that become unused because of a business reason are reimbursed with a short written explanation from the Department Head.

## 7. Claims

Travel claims follow the process, approval levels and time limits in FIN-REIM-001. Attach the approval email, hotel invoice and transport tickets.
