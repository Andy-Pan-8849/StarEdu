# Meals, Entertainment and Teaching Supplies Policy

## 1. Client and partner entertainment

Entertainment of employers, hiring partners and suppliers is reimbursed when:

1. The Campus Director approved it in advance, in writing.
2. The cost does not exceed **CNY 300 per person per meal**.
3. The claim lists the guests and the business purpose.

Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## 2. Team activities

Each department may spend up to **CNY 200 per employee per quarter** on team activities. The Department Head approves the plan. Cash gifts are not allowed.

## 3. Overtime meals and transport

An employee who works after 21:00 on a weekday at the request of a manager may claim CNY 40 for a meal and a taxi ride home. Weekend work approved by the Department Head follows the same rule.

## 4. Teaching supplies

Instructors may buy classroom supplies such as markers, cables, adapters and printed handouts.

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Spending without pre-approval | Up to CNY 500 per class group per month |
| Spending above that | Pre-approval from the Department Head |
| Equipment above CNY 2,000 per item | Treated as a campus asset, purchased through Procurement (see FIN-BUD-007) |

Supplies are claimed in the category TEACH with the invoice.

## 5. Student events

Hackathons, graduation events and employer visits are paid from the campus budget line "Events", not through personal claims. The Admissions and Student Services department head approves the plan and Finance assigns a project code.

## 6. Gifts

Gifts to clients or partners are limited to CNY 200 in value and need approval from the Campus Director.
