# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how employees claim business expenses at StarIT Education. Specific limits for travel are in FIN-REIM-002, and for meals, entertainment and teaching supplies in FIN-REIM-003. Invoice rules are in FIN-TAX-008.

## 2. Submitting a claim

1. Pay the expense with your own funds, or with a cash advance (section 6).
2. Collect the VAT invoice (fapiao) and any supporting document, such as a train ticket or approval email.
3. Submit the claim in the Finance portal, choosing the correct expense category.
4. Claims must be submitted **within 30 calendar days** after the expense date. Claims submitted after 30 days need approval from the Finance Manager. Claims older than 90 days are rejected.

## 3. Approval levels

| Claim amount (CNY) | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to 1,000 | Department Head |
| 1,001 to 10,000 | Campus Director |
| Above 10,000 | Regional VP and Finance Controller |

A claim that is split into several smaller claims to avoid a higher approval level will be rejected, and the employee may face disciplinary action.

## 4. Payment

After the final approval, Finance pays the claim within **7 business days** by bank transfer to the employee's payroll account. Claims approved after the 25th of a month may be paid in the first week of the next month.

## 5. Missing invoices

If a VAT invoice is lost, the employee may submit a Lost Receipt Form signed by the Campus Director. Each employee may use this form at most 2 times per year, and each claim is limited to CNY 500.

## 6. Cash advances

An employee with an approved business trip may request a cash advance of up to CNY 5,000. The advance must be settled within 14 days after the trip ends. An unsettled advance is deducted from the next payroll.

## 7. Certification and training claims

Subsidies for external certification (HR-TRN-004) are claimed in the category CERT and follow the same approval levels as other claims.

## 8. Prohibited expenses

Personal purchases, traffic fines, alcohol, gifts with a value above CNY 200, and any expense without a valid invoice (except under section 5) are not reimbursed.
