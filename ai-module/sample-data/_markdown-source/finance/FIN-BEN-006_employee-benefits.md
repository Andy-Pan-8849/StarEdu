# Employee Benefits Policy

## 1. Statutory benefits

StarIT pays social insurance and the housing fund according to the rules of the city of the employee's campus.

## 2. Supplementary medical insurance

Full-time employees who have completed probation are covered by a supplementary commercial medical insurance, with an annual coverage of **CNY 500,000**. Family members can be added at the employee's own cost.

## 3. Annual health check

Each employee receives a health check worth up to **CNY 800** per year at a partner clinic. Employees book the check in the HR portal. There is nothing to claim.

## 4. Meal subsidy

A meal subsidy is paid per working day, with the monthly payroll. The standard amount is CNY 25. Some campuses have a higher amount because of local costs:

| Campus | Meal subsidy per working day (CNY) |
|---|---|
| Beijing | 35 |
| Shanghai | 35 |
| Shenzhen | 35 |
| Guangzhou | 30 |
| Hangzhou, Chengdu, Wuhan, Xi'an | 25 |

## 5. Relocation support

An instructor at level I2 or above who relocates more than 500 km to join a Tier 1 campus receives a housing allowance of **CNY 1,500 per month for the first 12 months**.

## 6. Festival gifts and life events

- Gift of CNY 300 value for the Spring Festival and the Mid-Autumn Festival
- Marriage gift of CNY 500
- Birth gift of CNY 800 per child

## 7. Staff tuition discount

Employees and their spouses and children receive a **30 percent discount** on the tuition of one StarIT bootcamp per person. The discount cannot be combined with other discounts.

## 8. Other benefits

Annual leave and family leave are described in HR-LEV-007. Training subsidies are described in HR-TRN-004.
