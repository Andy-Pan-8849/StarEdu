# Payroll Policy

## 1. Pay date

Salary for the previous calendar month is paid on the **10th of each month**. If the 10th is a weekend or public holiday, salary is paid on the last business day before the 10th.

## 2. Payroll cut-off

Attendance, leave and overload data are closed on the 25th of each month. Changes after the cut-off are processed in the next payroll. Changes of bank account must reach Finance before the 20th.

## 3. Pay structure

| Group | Fixed base | Performance pay |
|---|---|---|
| Instructors | 70 percent of target monthly pay | 30 percent |
| Other staff | 85 percent of target monthly pay | 15 percent |

The performance pay is paid quarterly according to the rating in HR-PERF-006:

| Rating | Share of target performance pay paid |
|---|---|
| A | 120 percent |
| B+ | 110 percent |
| B | 100 percent |
| C | 70 percent |

## 4. Overtime

- **Instructors:** teaching beyond 16 hours per week is paid at CNY 120 per teaching hour (HR-WRK-008). The weekly maximum is 24 teaching hours.
- **Non-exempt staff** (for example operations and student services assistants): 1.5 times the hourly rate on weekdays, 2 times on weekends and 3 times on public holidays. Overtime must be approved in advance.

## 5. Payslips and corrections

Payslips are available in the HR portal on the pay date. An employee who finds an error must report it within 30 days. An underpayment above CNY 500 is corrected within 5 business days in an off-cycle payment; smaller corrections are paid in the next payroll.

## 6. Deductions

Salary is paid after the personal share of social insurance and housing fund and individual income tax. Unsettled cash advances (FIN-REIM-001) are also deducted.

## 7. New joiners and leavers

For employees who join or leave during a month, salary is calculated pro rata by calendar days.
