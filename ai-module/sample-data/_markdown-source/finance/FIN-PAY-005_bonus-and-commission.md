# Bonus and Commission Policy (Confidential)

This document is restricted to Finance and HR staff.

## 1. Annual bonus

All full-time employees who have worked at least 6 months by 31 December are eligible.

**Formula:** annual bonus = monthly base salary x rating multiplier x company factor.

| Annual rating (HR-PERF-006) | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| A | 1.5 |
| B+ | 1.2 |
| B | 1.0 |
| C | 0 |

The company factor is set by the Board in January and lies between 0.5 and 1.5. The bonus is paid with the February payroll.

## 2. Admissions commission

Admissions advisors earn **3 percent of the net tuition collected** for each student they enroll. Net tuition excludes refunds and discounts.

- The commission is paid in the month after the 14-day refund window of the student closes.
- If a student withdraws within 30 days after the program starts, the commission is clawed back in full from the next payroll.
- Team leads earn an extra 0.5 percent on the net tuition of their team.

## 3. Referral bonus

Referral bonuses follow HR-REC-001: CNY 3,000 for instructor and engineering hires, CNY 1,500 for other roles, paid with payroll after the referred employee completes 90 days.

## 4. Spot awards

Campus Directors may grant spot awards between CNY 500 and CNY 2,000 for exceptional work, up to 3 awards per campus per quarter. Awards are paid with the next payroll.

## 5. Tax

All bonuses and commissions are subject to individual income tax, withheld by Finance.
