# Campus Budget and Procurement Policy

This policy is for Campus Directors, Department Heads and Finance staff.

## 1. Fiscal year and budget cycle

The fiscal year is the calendar year. Campus budget proposals are submitted in October. The CFO approves the budgets in December. Budgets are reviewed every quarter.

## 2. Budget lines

Each campus budget has the lines: Payroll, Marketing, Facilities, Equipment, Teaching Supplies and Events.

## 3. Variance rules

| Variance against the quarterly budget | Required action |
|---|---|
| Up to 10 percent | None |
| More than 10 percent | Written explanation to Finance |
| More than 20 percent | CFO approval before any new spending in that line |

## 4. Procurement thresholds

| Purchase value per item or contract (CNY) | Process |
|---|---|
| Below 5,000 | Direct purchase by the department |
| 5,000 to 50,000 | At least three written quotes, approved by the Campus Director |
| Above 50,000 | Tender organized by the Procurement team |

## 5. Capital items

An item above **CNY 20,000** is a capital item. It needs approval from the Campus Director and the Finance Controller. Capital items above CNY 100,000 need approval from the CFO.

## 6. Year-end freeze

No new spending commitments are made in the last 5 business days of the fiscal year, unless the CFO approves.

## 7. Vendor rules

New vendors must be registered by Procurement with a valid business license and tax registration. Vendors related to employees must be declared (see HR-REC-001, conflict of interest).
