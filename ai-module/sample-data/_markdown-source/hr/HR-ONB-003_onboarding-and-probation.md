# Onboarding and Probation Policy

## 1. Probation length

| Role | Probation |
|---|---|
| Instructors and non-management staff | 3 months |
| Managers (levels M1 and above) | 6 months |

Probation may be extended once, by up to 1 month, if the manager and HR agree that more time is needed. Salary during probation is 100 percent of the contract salary.

## 2. First week

- A mentor is assigned in week 1. The mentor must be at the same level or senior to the new hire and must not be the direct manager.
- HR runs a half-day orientation on company policies, the HR portal and the finance system.
- IT issues a laptop and accounts. Technical support guides are listed in the TS- series of documents.

## 3. Instructor onboarding

A new instructor must complete the following before teaching alone:

1. The 40-hour teaching certification described in HR-TRN-004.
2. Observation of 6 classes taught by experienced instructors.
3. Two supervised classes, each followed by written feedback from the mentor.

Until these steps are done, the instructor is listed as "in training" and does not carry a full teaching load.

## 4. Probation reviews

Reviews take place on day 30, day 60 and day 80. The final review decides whether the employment is confirmed. HR issues the confirmation letter within 5 business days after the final review.

## 5. Notice periods

- During probation an employee may leave with 3 days of written notice.
- After confirmation the notice period is 30 days.
- The company may end employment during probation with 3 days of notice if the role requirements are not met.
