# Promotion and Career Ladder Policy

## 1. Career ladders

**Instructor track**

| Level | Title |
|---|---|
| I1 | Junior Instructor |
| I2 | Instructor |
| I3 | Senior Instructor |
| I4 | Lead Instructor |
| I5 | Principal Instructor |

**Management track**

| Level | Title |
|---|---|
| M1 | Team Lead |
| M2 | Department Head |
| M3 | Campus Director |

## 2. Promotion cycles

Promotion panels meet twice a year, in **April and October**. Applications are due 4 weeks before the panel meets. Out-of-cycle promotion is only possible with approval from the VP Academic for instructors, or the VP Human Resources for other staff.

## 3. General eligibility

An employee can apply when all of the following are true:

1. Time in the current level is at least 12 months (9 months for I1 to I2).
2. The performance rating is B+ or higher in the last two consecutive review cycles (see HR-PERF-006).
3. The average student satisfaction score over the previous two terms is at least 4.5 out of 5 (instructors only).
4. The required teaching certificate for the target level is held (see HR-TRN-004).

## 4. Additional requirements by level

| Promotion | Additional requirement |
|---|---|
| I2 to I3 | STC-2 certificate |
| I3 to I4 | Led the development of at least one course module, and mentored at least 2 instructors; STC-3 certificate |
| I4 to I5 | Authored a published course or a company-wide teaching standard; panel includes the Academic Dean and two Principal Instructors |
| Instructor to M1 | At least 24 months at level I3 or above, and a leadership interview |

## 5. Panel and outcome

The panel has three members. Decisions are communicated within 10 business days after the panel meets. A successful promotion brings a base salary increase of **8 to 15 percent**, effective on the first day of the month after the decision. Unsuccessful applicants receive written feedback and may re-apply in the next cycle.
