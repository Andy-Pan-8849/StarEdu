# StarIT Education Company Profile and Organization

*All names, numbers and policies in this document set are fictional and were synthesized for demonstration. They are inspired by common practice in the IT training industry and do not describe any real company.*

## 1. About StarIT Education

StarIT Education (Chinese name: Xingchen IT Jiaoyu) is a vocational IT training company founded in 2012 and headquartered in Chengdu. It runs full-time bootcamps that prepare career changers and graduates for software jobs. In 2025 the company employed about 1,100 people and trained about 14,000 students.

## 3. Programs

- Java Full-Stack Bootcamp (16 weeks)
- Python and AI Engineering Bootcamp (16 weeks)
- TypeScript Full-Stack Bootcamp (16 weeks)
- Data Engineering Bootcamp (20 weeks)
- Software Testing Bootcamp (12 weeks)

## 4. Departments

Each campus has the following departments, all reporting to the Campus Director: Academic (instructors and curriculum), Admissions and Student Services, Career Services, and Campus Operations. Group-level functions are Human Resources, Finance, IT and Technical Support, and the Academic Office led by the VP Academic.
