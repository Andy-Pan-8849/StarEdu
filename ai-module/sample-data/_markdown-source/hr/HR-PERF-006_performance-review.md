# Performance Review Policy

## 1. Review cycle

Performance is reviewed every quarter. The fourth-quarter review also serves as the annual review that drives the annual bonus (see FIN-PAY-005).

## 2. Rating scale and target distribution

| Rating | Meaning | Target share of employees |
|---|---|---|
| A | Outstanding | 10 percent |
| B+ | Exceeds expectations | 25 percent |
| B | Meets expectations | 50 percent |
| C | Below expectations | 15 percent |

Campus Directors may deviate from the target distribution by at most 5 percentage points per rating, and must explain the deviation to HR.

## 3. Instructor scorecard

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Student satisfaction (end-of-module survey, 5-point scale) | 30 percent |
| Teaching quality (class observation by a senior instructor, twice per term) | 25 percent |
| Student outcomes (assignment pass rate and employment rate of the cohort) | 25 percent |
| Contribution (curriculum work, mentoring, team collaboration) | 20 percent |

## 4. Other staff

Staff in admissions, career services, operations and group functions are rated on goals agreed at the start of the quarter, using an objectives-based scorecard approved by the department head.

## 5. Consequences

- Two consecutive C ratings lead to a 60-day performance improvement plan (PIP).
- If the PIP goals are not met, employment may be ended with 30 days of notice.
- Ratings of B+ or higher in the last two cycles are one condition for promotion (see HR-PRM-005).

## 6. Appeals

An employee may appeal a rating within 10 business days by writing to the HR Business Partner. A panel of two department heads from another campus reviews the appeal and decides within 15 business days.
