# Recruitment Policy (version 1.0, superseded)

This version was replaced by version 2.1 on 2026-01-01 and is kept for reference only.

## 1. Requisition approval

The hiring manager submits a requisition. The Campus Director approves it. Finance confirms budget.

## 2. Posting

Positions are advertised externally and internally at the same time. Postings stay open for at least 10 calendar days.

## 3. Interview stages

All roles go through resume screening, a hiring manager interview and a final HR interview. Instructor candidates also give a teaching demo of 20 minutes.

## 4. Offers

The offer is sent within 10 business days after the final interview. An offer is valid for 5 calendar days. A background check is required for all hires.

## 5. Employee referral bonus

The referral bonus is **CNY 2,000** for instructor and engineering hires and **CNY 1,000** for all other roles. The bonus is paid after the referred employee has completed 90 days of employment.

## 6. Records

Candidate records are kept for 24 months.
