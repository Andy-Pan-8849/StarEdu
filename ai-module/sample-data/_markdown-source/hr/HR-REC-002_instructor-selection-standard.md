# Instructor Selection Standard (Confidential)

This document is restricted to HR staff and the Academic Dean. Do not share scoring details or salary bands with candidates or other employees.

## 1. Scoring rubric

Every instructor candidate is scored out of 100 by the interview panel.

| Dimension | Weight | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Technical depth | 40 | Technical interview, coding exercise |
| Teaching demo | 30 | 30-minute demo of a curriculum topic, observed by two instructors |
| Communication | 20 | Clarity, pacing, handling of questions |
| Culture fit | 10 | Final HR interview |

## 2. Pass rules

- The pass threshold is **75 points** for levels I2 and above.
- For level I1 (Junior Instructor, fresh graduates or up to 2 years of experience) the threshold is 70 points, and a mentoring plan is mandatory.
- A candidate who scores below 50 percent of the maximum in any single dimension is rejected, whatever the total.

## 3. Level placement

| Total score | Placement |
|---|---|
| 70 to 74 | I1 Junior Instructor (only with a mentoring plan) |
| 75 to 84 | I2 Instructor |
| 85 to 92 | I3 Senior Instructor, if the candidate has at least 5 years of relevant experience; otherwise I2 |
| 93 and above | Refer to the Academic Dean for a Lead Instructor (I4) review |

## 4. Base salary bands

Monthly base salary in CNY for Tier 1 campuses. Tier 2 campuses pay 85 percent of the band.

| Level | Minimum | Maximum |
|---|---|---|
| I1 Junior Instructor | 12,000 | 15,000 |
| I2 Instructor | 15,000 | 20,000 |
| I3 Senior Instructor | 20,000 | 28,000 |
| I4 Lead Instructor | 28,000 | 38,000 |
| I5 Principal Instructor | 38,000 | 50,000 |

An offer above the band maximum requires approval from the VP Human Resources, as stated in HR-REC-001.

## 5. Panel composition

The panel has three members: one instructor of level I3 or above, one member of the Academic Office, and one HR representative who does not score technical depth.
