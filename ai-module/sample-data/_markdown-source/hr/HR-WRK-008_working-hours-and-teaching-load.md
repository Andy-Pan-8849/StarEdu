# Working Hours and Teaching Load Policy

## 1. Standard hours

The standard working week is 40 hours, Monday to Friday. Group-function staff at the headquarters may work from home one day per week with the approval of their manager. Instructors work on campus.

## 2. Instructor teaching load

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Standard teaching load | 16 teaching hours per week |
| Remaining time | About 24 hours for preparation, tutoring, office hours and curriculum work |
| Maximum teaching load | 24 teaching hours per week |

Teaching beyond 16 hours per week is overload. Overload is paid at **CNY 120 per hour** with the next payroll (see FIN-PAY-004). Overload above 24 hours per week is not allowed, even with the instructor's consent.

## 3. Weekend and evening classes

Weekend classes are voluntary for instructors with a full weekday load. An instructor who teaches on a weekend receives a compensatory day off within 30 days, or may choose overload pay instead if the weekend hours push the week above 16 teaching hours.

## 4. Class schedule

Classes run in three blocks: 09:00 to 12:00, 14:00 to 17:30 and 18:30 to 21:30. Instructors must be in the classroom 10 minutes before the start.

## 5. Office hours

Each instructor holds at least 4 office hours per week, announced to students on the first day of the module.

## 6. Records

Teaching hours are recorded by the Academic department and used for overload payment and for the performance scorecard (HR-PERF-006).
