# Recruitment Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets out how StarIT Education opens positions, selects candidates and extends offers. It applies to all campuses and to all full-time roles. It replaces version 1.0, which was in force from 2024-03-01 to 2025-12-31.

## 2. Requisition approval

1. The hiring manager submits a requisition in the HR portal.
2. The Campus Director and the HR Business Partner approve it.
3. Finance confirms that the headcount is inside the approved campus budget.
4. Instructor requisitions also need approval from the Academic Dean.

A requisition that is not approved within 10 business days is automatically escalated to the VP Human Resources.

## 3. Posting

Every position is posted internally for at least 5 business days before it is advertised externally. External postings stay open for at least 14 calendar days unless the position is filled earlier.

## 4. Interview stages

| Role type | Stages |
|---|---|
| Instructor | Resume screening, technical interview (60 minutes), teaching demo (30 minutes), final HR interview (30 minutes) |
| Student services, admissions, operations | Resume screening, hiring manager interview, final HR interview |
| Group functions (HR, Finance, IT) | Resume screening, functional interview, final HR interview |

HR completes resume screening within 3 business days of receiving an application. Instructor candidates are scored with the Instructor Selection Standard (HR-REC-002), which is restricted to HR staff.

## 5. Offers and background checks

- The offer is sent within 5 business days after the final interview.
- An offer is valid for 7 calendar days.
- A background check is required for all hires. For instructors, education verification is also required.
- Offers above the salary band of the role need approval from the VP Human Resources.

## 6. Employee referral bonus

Employees may refer candidates. The referral bonus is **CNY 3,000** for instructor and engineering hires and **CNY 1,500** for all other roles. The bonus is paid with payroll after the referred employee has completed 90 days of employment. HR staff and the hiring manager of the position are not eligible for the bonus.

## 7. Conflict of interest

Close relatives must not work in the same reporting line. Candidates must disclose such relationships during the interview process.

## 8. Records

Candidate records of people who were not hired are kept for 12 months and then deleted.
