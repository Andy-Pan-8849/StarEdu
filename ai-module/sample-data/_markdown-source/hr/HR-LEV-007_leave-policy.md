# Leave Policy

## 1. Annual leave

Annual leave depends on years of service at StarIT Education, counted on 1 January:

| Years of service | Paid annual leave per year |
|---|---|
| 1 to under 5 years | 7 days |
| 5 to under 10 years | 10 days |
| 10 years and more | 15 days |

Employees in their first year receive 1 day per completed 2 months of service. Public holidays are in addition to annual leave.

Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March.

## 2. Sick leave

Employees have up to 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required when sick leave lasts more than 2 consecutive working days. Longer sick leave is handled under local statutory rules.

## 3. Family leave

- Marriage leave: 10 days
- Paternity leave: 15 days, to be taken within 6 months of the birth
- Bereavement leave for a direct family member: 3 days

Maternity leave follows the local statutory provision of the campus city.

## 4. Personal leave

Personal leave is unpaid and needs the approval of the direct manager. It is limited to 5 days per year.

## 5. Rules for instructors

Teaching sessions cannot be cancelled. An instructor who wants leave on a day with scheduled classes must:

1. Submit the request at least 14 days in advance, in the HR portal.
2. Propose a substitute instructor who is qualified for the course.
3. Get approval from the department head, who confirms the substitute.

Emergency sick leave is exempt from the 14-day rule. The instructor informs the department head before 7:30 on the day, and the department head arranges cover.

## 6. How to request

All leave is requested in the HR portal. Leave of 3 days or more needs approval from the department head; shorter leave needs approval from the direct manager.
