# Instructor Training and Certification Policy

## 1. Annual professional development

| Group | Required hours per calendar year |
|---|---|
| Instructors | 40 |
| Other staff | 24 |

Hours can be earned through internal workshops, external courses, conferences and approved self-study with a written summary.

## 2. Teaching certification

All instructors must hold the StarIT Teaching Certificate. It has three levels:

| Level | Content | Required for |
|---|---|---|
| STC-1 | 40-hour Train-the-Trainer program (5 days), classroom practice, assessment | All new instructors before solo teaching |
| STC-2 | Advanced course design and assessment, 24 hours | Promotion to I3 Senior Instructor |
| STC-3 | Curriculum leadership and mentoring, 24 hours | Promotion to I4 Lead Instructor |

The Train-the-Trainer program runs at least once per quarter at the Chengdu headquarters and online.

## 3. External certification subsidy

The company pays **80 percent of the exam or course fee, up to CNY 5,000 per calendar year** for certifications related to the instructor's teaching area, for example cloud, Java, Python or data certificates.

Rules:

1. The manager must approve the certification before the employee registers.
2. The subsidy is paid after the employee passes and submits the certificate and receipt. The claim is made through the expense process in FIN-REIM-001.
3. The employee commits to stay for 12 months after payment. If they leave earlier, the subsidy is repaid pro rata by remaining months.

University degrees and language courses are not covered.

## 4. Conferences

Each instructor may attend one external conference per year at company cost. The Campus Director approves the request. Attendees present a 30-minute internal summary within 30 days.
